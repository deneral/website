Commissions — FAQ

Are you available for commissions?

Yes — currently open. Reach out at studio@paolointerno.com to discuss your project, timeline, and budget.

What kind of illustration work do you do?

Detailed, narrative-driven fantasy landscape and environment illustration for games (tabletop RPGs, video games) and publishing. Focused on composition, lighting, and readability at multiple scales within production constraints.

How can I hire you as a freelance illustrator?

Email studio@paolointerno.com with a brief description of your project. Paolo works with clients worldwide and is experienced with game and publishing production pipelines.
